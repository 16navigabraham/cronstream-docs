# Cronstream Protocol Lab

**Open-source Uniswap v4 hook infrastructure. Built in public.**

Cronstream builds and ships v4 hooks that solve real liquidity problems — starting with reward aggregation and growing into a suite of open primitives.

---

## Current Build: Reward Aggregator Hook

Protocols spend millions on fragmented liquidity mining. LPs claim from ten different contracts. Neither side wins.

Cronstream's Reward Aggregator hook lets multiple protocols stream incentives directly into a v4 pool. LPs earn from every source automatically — no separate staking, no manual claims. One pool, aggregated rewards, continuous settlement.
