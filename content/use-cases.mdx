# Use Cases

Cronstream is not a general-purpose hook. It solves one specific problem: yield-bearing and rebasing assets that lose their distribution mechanism inside a Uniswap v4 pool. These are the protocols and asset classes where that problem is most acute.

---

## Permian Labs — sUSDai (AI Infrastructure Finance)

**Protocol:** Permian Labs  
**Asset:** sUSDai — a staked, yield-bearing synthetic dollar (ERC-4626)  
**Backing:** Real-world GPU infrastructure loans and AI compute cluster cash flows  
**Funding:** $13M Series A led by Framework Ventures, backed by Coinbase Ventures

### The mechanism

Permian Labs issues two tokens:

- **USDai** — a synthetic dollar pegged to $1, used for deep liquidity
- **sUSDai** — the staked, yield-bearing version. Yield is generated by actual loan interest repayments from AI companies and GPU data centers borrowing against physical hardware

sUSDai is an ERC-4626 vault token. Its share value updates dynamically as real-world loan repayments flow in — meaning its underlying value per token increases over time, rather than the supply rebasing.

### Where the yield breaks

When an institutional LP deposits sUSDai into a Uniswap v4 pool, the PoolManager tracks balances as static integers. It has no awareness that sUSDai's underlying value is increasing with each AI infrastructure loan repayment. The growing asset-level yield creates an unaccounted surplus inside the pool — cash flows from physical GPU fleets sitting as stranded float, inaccessible to the LPs who provided the liquidity.

The alternative is what Permian Labs would otherwise have to build: a centralized off-chain script that snapshots LP positions, calculates pro-rata shares, and runs a manual batch distribution. This is exactly the custodial, operationally fragile model that Cronstream eliminates.

### The Cronstream integration

A Cronstream hook deployed on sUSDai/USDai pairs creates the automated distribution rail:

1. `afterAddLiquidity` and `afterRemoveLiquidity` track each LP's share of the pool in real time
2. As sUSDai loan repayments flow in and the vault's share value increases, the hook detects the growing balance surplus against the pool's last recorded baseline
3. When the surplus crosses the distribution threshold, the keeper calls `distributeYield()`
4. The AI infrastructure revenue — denominated in sUSDai — is pushed directly to every LP's wallet in a single atomic transaction

LPs in a Permian Labs pool with Cronstream receive GPU infrastructure yield automatically, with no claim transaction, no centralized operator, and no custodial exposure.

<Callout type="info">
  sUSDai is an ERC-4626 vault token rather than a rebasing token. Cronstream's surplus detection via `poolManager.sync()` captures both rebase-style and vault-style yield accumulation, making it compatible with the full spectrum of yield-bearing asset architectures.
</Callout>

## Ondo Finance — USDY

**Asset:** USDY — tokenized U.S. Treasury yield, $2.16B market cap  
**Mechanism:** Rebasing — supply increases daily as Treasury yield accrues  
**Problem:** Each daily rebase increases the PoolManager's USDY balance without updating its internal reserves. Surplus accumulates as unassigned float.

Cronstream detects the post-rebase surplus via `afterDonate` / `poolManager.sync()` and distributes it to LPs automatically.

## Mountain Protocol — USDM

**Asset:** USDM — yield-bearing stablecoin, $1.1B AUM  
**Mechanism:** Rebasing — balances increase daily for all holders  
**Problem:** Same as USDY. The PoolManager's USDM balance grows daily but is not attributed to any LP.

USDM pools with Cronstream deliver daily stablecoin yield directly to LP wallets without any manual intervention.

## BlackRock — BUIDL

**Asset:** BUIDL — tokenized Treasury fund, $2.4B AUM  
**Mechanism:** Dividend — weekly yield distribution to registered holders  
**Problem:** When BUIDL is held by the PoolManager as pool liquidity, the dividend distribution goes to the pool contract address, not to individual LPs. LPs receive nothing.

Cronstream intercepts the dividend landing in the pool, attributes it pro-rata across all active LP positions, and distributes it in the next distribution cycle.

## The broader market

Any ERC-4626 vault token, rebasing stablecoin, or dividend-paying asset deployed into a Uniswap v4 pool has this problem. Cronstream is the generalized solution — one hook deployment, any yield-bearing asset.

| Asset type | Examples | Yield mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Tokenized Treasuries | BUIDL, BENJI, USTB | Weekly/daily dividend |
| Rebasing stablecoins | USDY, USDM, stUSDT | Daily rebase |
| ERC-4626 vault tokens | sUSDai, sDAI, wstETH | Share value appreciation |
| Liquid staking tokens | stETH, rETH | Daily rebase / share value |
